Your weekly training rhythm.

Repeat this loop for each module. The goal is steady ball control: understand, copy, change, and build.

  1. First touch: Read one lesson and say the soccer analogy out loud in your own words.
  2. Passing drill: Type both examples by hand, then change player names, scores, or limits.
  3. Small-sided game: Finish the drill without looking at the solution for at least 10 minutes.
  4. Match review: Explain what broke, what fixed it, and what pattern you saw.
